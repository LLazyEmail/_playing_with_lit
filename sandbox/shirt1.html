Introducing the hnst-tee

Pictures by Birgit Sterckx

The hnst-tee is a heavy-weight and high-quality t-shirt made of the fashion industry’s waste.

Through recycling the industry’s cutting waste and dead-stock, we’re saving incredible amounts of land, water and virgin resources.

The tee is made of 80% recycled cotton and 20% organic cotton.
The boxy fit enhances the flattering flow of the fabric.

Honestly, these subtle high-quality details make it the perfect everyday t-shirt.
