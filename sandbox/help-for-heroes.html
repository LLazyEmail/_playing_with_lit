Hi Help for Heroes Supporter,


Earlier this month, we launched our 40,000 Strong campaign to highlight that in the last 20 years, almost 40,000 men and women have had to leave the Armed Forces due to injury or illness.


Every day, this number grows, with an average of seven people medically discharged every day since drawdown from Afghanistan.


Thousands are falling through gaps. We need your help today to fix these gaps and ensure the government improve the transition process for all wounded veterans.

This is why w e launched our 40,000 Strong installation at the beginning of October in Manchester’s Arndale Centre, which is now touring the UK.


You can own part of the installation by donating to receive your special edition 40,000 Strong boxed figure.
